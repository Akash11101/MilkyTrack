# MilkyTrack - Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 2026_

MilkyTrack is a personal milk-delivery ledger. **Your data stays on your phone.**

- **What the app stores:** customer names, phone numbers, addresses, delivery quantities, prices, paid marks and the UPI ID you enter. It is saved only in the app's private storage on your device.
- **What we collect:** nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers. We never receive your data.
- **Backups:** if Android's Google Drive backup is on, Android may back up the app's data to your own Google account. "Back up data" copies your data to the clipboard for you to keep wherever you choose.
- **Sharing:** the app shares data only when you tap a button yourself: WhatsApp reminders, UPI payment links, PDF bills and copied summaries. Those go to the app you choose.
- **Permissions:** the app asks for no sensitive permissions (no location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage).
- **Deleting your data:** remove a customer inside the app, or uninstall the app to erase everything stored on the device.
- **Children:** the app is not directed at children.
- **Changes:** if this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
